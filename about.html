10 years of Valley news and events

What started as a Facebook page for the upper Valley is now a full site, still run by a neighbor.

Ogden Valley Information & Events shares what's happening in Eden, Liberty and Huntsville: events, road and canyon heads-ups, local businesses and the people who make the Valley what it is. About 14,000 people follow along on Facebook.

For our 10th year we've grown into this site, with calendars you can add to your phone, a weekly newsletter, a monthly photo contest, and listings for Valley restaurants, businesses and artists.

We're online only. Event details come from organizers, so please check with them before you go.
