Valley sponsor spotlight

Yes, I Can Fix That

A family- and veteran-owned handyman business serving Ogden Valley and Weber County. Local roots, experienced hands.

Valley sponsor Yes, I Can Fix That is owned by Seth and Shauna Miller. Shauna also publishes this site, so we're telling you up front. Like every sponsor, it's labeled, and it never changes the order of listings, reviews or events.

Meet Seth

Local roots. Experienced hands.

Seth was raised in North Ogden and is a U.S. Marine Corps veteran with more than 20 years of hands-on experience fixing the things around the house that need fixing. Shauna grew up in North Ogden and raised their family here in Ogden Valley.

After years of running Yes, I Can Fix That in Florida, they've brought the business home to Utah, open year-round.

How they work

One job at a time

  • Your job gets their full attention. They never schedule two projects at once.
  • Straightforward quotes and on-time arrival
  • A clean job site when they leave
  • Veteran owned and insured
  • They don't sell any customer information

Where the name comes from

“Can we fix that?” “Yes, we can.”

Shauna's dad, Monte Carroll, grew up in Pleasant View. He was a union journeyman plumber who taught at Ogden Technical College and Salt Lake Community College, and on Saturdays his kids went along on jobs or helped with repairs around the house. When Seth and Shauna bought their first home, Monte taught Seth how to lay tile, replace doors and run trim. That's where the plans for this business began.

When Shauna's little brother Porter would look up and ask, “Can we fix that?” their dad always answered, “Yes, we can.” The company is named after those words.

What they fix

Doors

Sticking and misaligned interior doors, hardware, casing, weather stripping and thresholds. For a full exterior front door replacement, they'll connect you with a trusted local installer.

Drywall & texture

Holes and patches, texture matched to what's already there, and walls rebuilt after water or mold repair.

Flooring

Luxury vinyl plank (LVP), board replacements, transition trim, vinyl, carpet replacement and tile.

Carpentry & trim

Baseboards, crown molding, built-in shelving, custom woodwork, and cabinet and drawer repairs.

Mounting & small jobs

Shelves, grab bars, towel bars, curtain rods, furniture assembly, toilet tank repairs and small appliance repairs.

Outdoor upkeep

Gutter clearing, fence and gate repairs, mailbox replacement and general property maintenance.

Need something they don't do? From septic service to front door installs, they keep a list of local pros they've worked with and trust, and they'll point you to the right one.

New in Utah

The Learning Center: yes, you can fix that

Utah law doesn't allow handymen to replace plumbing or electrical fixtures like faucets, toilets, light fixtures and ceiling fans, but homeowners can do that work on their own homes. So Seth brings the tools and the know-how, and walks you through it step by step while you do the work. You save the cost of a plumber or electrician on the small jobs and know how to do it next time.

  • Private lesson in your home: $225 for up to 2 hours, $100 each hour after
  • Group classes: $30 per person, up to 7 people. Great for youth groups, neighbors and friends
  • Every graduate earns a Yes, I Can Fix That certificate
