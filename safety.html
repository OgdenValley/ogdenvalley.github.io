Show us your weather

Send your weather photo

Snow piling up in Eden? A rainbow over Pineview? Send a photo with the Weather photo choice. Tell us the town or a landmark and when you took it. We look at every photo before it goes up, and photos stay here for 7 days. Great shots can be picked for the monthly photo contest.

No faces, children or license plates, and no house numbers. See the community rules. By sending a photo you agree we may share it on this site, in the newsletter and on our Facebook and Instagram, with your first name or "a neighbor."
