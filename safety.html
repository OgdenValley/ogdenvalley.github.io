    Who to call

    Emergency: someone hurt, a crash, a fire, or a rock slide or animal blocking the road right now: call or text 911.

    Not an emergency

    Animals or loose livestock on the road, a stray dog, something to report that isn't urgent: Weber Area Dispatch non-emergency 801-395-8221. This is also how you reach county animal control officers.

    Rock slides, debris, potholes, signs down

    On Highway 39, 158, 162, Trappers Loop or the North Ogden Divide: tell UDOT with Click 'n Fix or call 801-965-4000. If it's blocking traffic, call 911.

    Moose, bears & cougars

    A moose in town, or a bear or cougar getting into trash, killing animals or acting aggressive: Utah DWR Northern Region (Ogden) 801-476-2740. Poaching: 800-662-3337 or text 847411. Never approach or herd a moose.

    Lost or found pets

    Weber County Animal Services 801-399-8244, 1373 N 750 W, Ogden. Open Mon–Fri noon to 5:30 p.m., Sat noon to 5 p.m. (adoptions), closed Sunday.

    Numbers from Weber Area 911, UDOT, Utah DWR and Weber County Animal Services, checked Oct. 22, 2026.

    Planned road work · Weber County Roads

    North Ogden Divide repair · Oct. 20

    Starts Tuesday, Oct. 20, around 8 a.m. Work lasts 1 to 2 days. Message boards go up Friday, Oct. 16.

    What: Weber County Roads is repairing failing asphalt on the North Ogden Divide.

    Where: The closure affects the east half of the road. Flaggers will run one-way traffic.

    When: Work starts around 8 a.m. so Weber High School students can get through first.

    Delays: Plan on about 5 minutes, with longer waits possible at busy times. Ogden Canyon and Trappers Loop are other routes.

    Source: Weber County Roads notice, "Repair Work on the North Ogden Divide Scheduled for October 20," as shared Oct. 13, 2026. Dates can change with weather.

    Planned road work · from the project's notice

    Ogden Canyon lane closure (SR-39)

    Project website
    New start date: as early as Monday, Nov. 2. UDOT now lists the closure starting Nov. 2 and running through April 2027 (it was first set for Oct. 26). Dates can still change, so check udottraffic.utah.gov before you drive.

    What: Crews will install pipeline along SR-39 in Ogden Canyon for Ogden City's Ogden Canyon 36-Inch Pipeline Replacement.

    Where: One lane closed for about a quarter mile, between The Oaks Restaurant and the Ogden Canyon Water Treatment Plant.

    When: As early as Monday, Nov. 2, 2026, through April 2027. One-way alternating traffic 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

    Delays: Expect 5 to 10 minutes. Leave extra time, or use Trappers Loop (SR-167).

    Questions: Project hotline 801-838-8365 (weekdays during work hours) or info@ogdencitypipeline.com.

    Source: Ogden Canyon 36-Inch Pipeline Replacement traffic notice, "Attention Motorists," October 2026. UDOT Traffic roadwork listing for SR-39 (upper Ogden Canyon, mileposts 12.9 to 13.3), checked Oct. 29, 2026. Hotline and email from ogdencitypipeline.com, checked Oct. 12, 2026.
