Huntsville's First Annual Christmas Parade

Tuesday, Dec. 1 · 6:30 PM, Huntsville

A brand-new holiday tradition from the Huntsville Town Traditions Committee. Businesses, families, church groups and neighbors are invited to decorate a float or dress up a vehicle. Or come cheer along the route.

  • Route: the same basic route as the July 4th parade, with the best viewing around the park. Afterward, the fun moves to the park stage with surprises for the kids.
  • Food and toy drive: bring canned food or new toys in their original packaging.
  • Road closure: 200 South (between Town Hall and the park) will be closed from 7500 East to 7350 East, so plan your parking.

Source: Huntsville Town, Nov. 3, and Mayor Richard Sorensen's Thanksgiving message, Nov. 23.

Drink of the week: Blackberry Citrus Spritz

One recipe, two ways: a cocktail for the grown-ups or a zero-proof mocktail for everyone else. It's bright, bubbly and a pretty color on the holiday table. Makes one drink, so it's easy to make to order.

📄 Download the printable recipe (PDF): easier to read, and ready to print for the fridge.

Base ingredients

  • ¼ cup fresh blackberries
  • 1 oz fresh blood orange juice or lime juice
  • ¾ oz honey or simple syrup
  • 3 oz sparkling water or club soda
  • Ice, plus extra berries and a sprig of mint or rosemary to garnish

Pick your version

  • Cocktail: add 1½ oz London dry gin, blanco tequila or vodka.
  • Zero-proof: add 1½ oz non-alcoholic botanical spirit, or use ginger beer.

How to make it

  1. Muddle: gently press the blackberries, citrus juice and honey or syrup in a shaker or the bottom of your glass.
  2. Stir and chill: add your spirit or zero-proof choice and crushed ice, and stir gently.
  3. Top and garnish: fill with sparkling water, add fresh berries and herbs, and serve cold.

Enjoy responsibly, and plan a sober driver before anyone heads down the canyon. Got a favorite holiday drink or recipe? Send it to us for Community Cooking at ogdenvalleyevent.com.

Road work

Ogden Canyon lane closure (SR-39)

One lane is closed on about a quarter mile of SR-39 between The Oaks and the Ogden Canyon Water Treatment Plant while crews replace a 100-year-old water pipeline. The closure started Nov. 2 and is set to run through April 2027. Plan a few extra minutes, especially on busy holiday and ski days. Source: UDOT and Ogden City, via KUTV.

Huntsville Town notes

Water shutdown coming next week

Huntsville Town's new waterline is nearly finished: almost 4,000 feet of new pipe is in and has passed both required water quality tests. Connecting it to the existing system will take a water shutdown of about one day, planned for after Thanksgiving, likely next week. The town will send a separate notice once the date is set. Sign up for town email and text alerts at HuntsvilleUtah.gov so you don't miss it.

A separate water leak shut off Huntsville's water on Monday, Nov. 23. The town says the leak was fixed and the water was back on that afternoon.

Remembering three Huntsville neighbors

In his Thanksgiving message, Mayor Richard Sorensen remembered three longtime Huntsville residents who died recently: Bill Wangsgard, a lifelong resident, former Town Council member and Grand Marshal of the 2022 July 4th parade; Paul Newey, a Huntsville native who served as a Weber County deputy and later Chief Deputy Sheriff, and on the Town Council and Planning Commission; and Jim Poulter, a fishing and hunting guide the mayor called probably the best fisherman ever to cast a line in local waters. Our thoughts are with all three families.

Source: Huntsville Town alerts and Mayor Sorensen's Thanksgiving message, Nov. 23.
