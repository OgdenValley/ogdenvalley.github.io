How it works

Free to be featured

Each teacher gets a feature article about who they are, how they learned, and their business. It stays up after the class so people keep finding them.

Signing up

Straight to the teacher

Each class lists the teacher's own sign-up link or phone number. They set the date, place, size and any cost for supplies.

Safety

Checked credentials

For skills like canning and food preservation, teachers must be trained through USU Extension and teach tested methods. We check before a class goes up.

Teacher features

Have a skill to share?

Canning, bread, quilting, fly tying, gardening at our elevation, first aid: if you know it, the Valley wants to learn it. Being featured is free.
