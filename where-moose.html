Where's the Moose?

Meet Ben Lomoose, the Ogden Valley moose, named after Ben Lomond Peak. Ben rode the train to Union Station in Ogden in his gold conductor's cap and blue bandana. Can you find him, and everything else on the list? Tap each one when you spot it. Littles get picture clues; bigger kids get a longer list.
