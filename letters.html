Letters from readers

How letters are chosen
  • The editor chooses which letters run and may shorten them for length. We don't change what you mean.
  • Before your first letter runs, we email you to confirm you wrote it.
  • No personal attacks, name-calling, or claims about a person that aren't true. Letters like that don't run.
  • Talk about decisions and ideas, not people's private lives.
  • Letters show the writer's own views, not the views of Ogden Valley Info & Events.
  • Phone numbers and street addresses are never printed.
