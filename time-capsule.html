Where these stories come from
  • Each capsule comes from a newspaper page in Utah Digital Newspapers, a free archive from the University of Utah’s J. Willard Marriott Library. Every capsule links to its original page.
  • The library lists these issues as “No Copyright – United States.” We tell each story in our own words, quote only short lines, and credit the paper and date.
  • We leave out the names of private people in arrests or illness, even in old stories.
