What sets off "briefing needed"

  • Snow in the Valley forecast (Eden, Liberty and Huntsville area)
  • A hard freeze: a low of 25° or colder
  • Two days in a row with a 70% or higher chance of rain or snow
  • Any National Weather Service warning for Weber County

A new draft is made every Monday morning, and again whenever a new reason shows up. Drafts use only numbers from the National Weather Service forecast. Read it over before it goes out.
